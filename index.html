Privacy Policy

Capacity Gravity: Storage & Media Cleaner

Last Updated: November 4, 2026

Zero Data Commitment: Capacity Gravity is built on an absolute zero-data, on-device architecture. The application physically contains NO internet permission (android.permission.INTERNET is absent from the manifest). We collect, transmit, store, track, and sell ZERO bytes of user data.

1. Local-Only Execution (Zero Network Access)

Capacity Gravity operates completely air-gapped from the internet. The application cannot establish socket connections, cannot reach remote web servers, and contains no analytics engines, advertising trackers, crash-reporting SDKs, or third-party telemetry libraries.

2. Data Collection and Storage

  • Personal Data: None collected. No accounts, emails, passwords, names, or phone numbers are ever requested.
  • Photos & Videos: Media files are analyzed strictly on-device in volatile CPU memory using local difference hashing (dHash). No images or videos are ever uploaded, synced, or backed up to remote servers.
  • Excluded Scan Folders & Settings: User-defined excluded folders and settings are stored locally on your device via Android Jetpack Preferences DataStore and are never transmitted off the device.
  • Local Hash Cache: A lightweight SQLite cache of 64-bit image fingerprints is maintained strictly on-device to accelerate subsequent scans. It contains zero image bytes, thumbnails, or personal information, and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

3. Device Permissions Explanation

Capacity Gravity requests the minimum local permissions necessary to provide device storage cleanup:

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_MEDIA_AUDIO: Required to scan media directories, generate local thumbnails, and compute visual hash signatures to identify burst duplicates.
  • MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Optional / Elevated): Required on Android 11+ strictly for deep disk scanning to locate unindexed orphaned downloads, messaging media dumps, and stale documents.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Capacity Gravity does not store copies of your files. When you choose to purge a file, the action is submitted directly to the Android OS confirmation dialog or executed locally on your storage. Deletions are permanent and executed locally.

5. Children's Privacy

Capacity Gravity does not collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. It is safe for all audiences.
